### ARCH-073 — Exercise drawings: Everkinetic only, two frames, shown in the Cues panel, credited on /credits
Date: 2026-11-14 | Status: Accepted (founder direction, 2026-11-14) | Engine unchanged, no schema change | Reversible: Yes (delete `public/exercises/` and the component).
Follows ARCH-072 (cues). Replaces the earlier plan of yuhonas/free-exercise-db images, whose provenance was unverified (PROJECT_STATE, 2026-10-22).

1. **Source.** `github.com/everkinetic/data` at commit `446bb9a3d0c3beb6b84f7c9d77dfc8af707a2ab6`, declared CC BY-SA 4.0 by the repository (the Internet Archive's copy of the original site is titled "Creative Commons Exercise Images" but does not state a version). Only `src/exercises/` (vector pairs) and `src/images-web/` are read; the `images-ai` and `images-png-unknown` folders, whose provenance is not stated, are never used. No bryllim images, no RepDB.
2. **Matching.** `apps/web/scripts/illustrations/mapping.ts` maps 72 of the 110 exercises. `exact` = same movement and equipment; `near` = same movement with a stated difference (11, each with a note, listed in the PR). A movement Everkinetic does not draw has no picture (38 gaps, accepted): a wrong picture is worse than none. Donkey Calf Raise was matched and dropped (its drawing has a second person on the lifter's back).
3. **Conversion.** `build.ts --source <checkout>` refuses any commit but the pinned one, rasterises vectors at 300 dpi, fits each frame in a 480 px white square and writes WebP (72 exercises, 144 files, 1.7 MB, none over 16 KB) plus `src/lib/exerciseIllustrations.json`. These are the only changes, and `/credits` says so.
4. **Display.** Inside the Cues panel (so the collapsed card and the 375 px fold are unchanged): a two-frame crossfade, three cycles of 1.6 s (4.8 s, under the five seconds that would need a pause control), then rest on the first frame. With `prefers-reduced-motion` both frames show side by side and nothing animates (this is not the real GIF the founder first wanted; no free animated source exists; **commissioning about 10 real GIFs is a future option if users complain**). Frames are not called start and end: Everkinetic's first frame is the bottom of some lifts and the top of others.
5. **Credit.** `/credits` (public; linked from the landing page and from a caption under each drawing) names Everkinetic and Greg Priday, the license, the changes, and the matching; it says "the training app" until the product name (D4) is decided. `public/exercises/ATTRIBUTION.md` carries the same beside the files.
6. **ShareAlike.** The converted images are adaptations and are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0 (the files are public; the repository is public). ShareAlike attaches to the images, not to the app's code. This is a reading of the license, not legal advice.
7. **Regenerating.** Clone everkinetic/data, `git checkout 446bb9a`, then `pnpm --filter web exec tsx scripts/illustrations/build.ts --source <clone>`. The test `exerciseIllustrations.test.ts` fails if the mapping, manifest, files, seed names or credits copy disagree.
