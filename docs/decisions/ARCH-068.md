### ARCH-068 — /check: the once-a-week "Program observation" (unscored)
Date: 2026-11-11 | Status: Accepted (founder decision, 2026-11-11) | Reversible: Yes. A display note beside the assessment; nothing is stored, `AssessmentSnapshot` is untouched, and removing the `observation` prop removes it.
Addendum to ARCH-065 (which rejected a frequency *finding*) and ARCH-067. Session G.

Context: both independent audits (2026-11-08) said the checker never says what a coach says first on a bro split or a once-a-week PPL: each muscle is trained once a week. The engine's FREQUENCY axis already exists, but it measures how well a muscle's dose is spread under about 10 sets a session; it does not call once a week a defect, because the profile's Tier 1-2 claim is that frequency has no independent effect on growth once weekly volume is equal (Schoenfeld 2019; Pelland 2026). A scored finding would contradict that claim.

Decision: state the fact, tied to what it means for this program, and do not score it.
1. **Detection.** Of the muscles the program trains (at least one exposure), 80% or more have exactly one exposure a week. Exposures and weekly sets are the FREQUENCY axis's own (`muscleExposures`, extracted from `computeFrequencyAxis` with identical output), scaled by `runsPerWeek`, so a program run on more days than listed does not fire. **Deviation from the founder's wording ("of goal-scope muscles"):** the denominator is the muscles the program *trains*, not all 11. The audit's bro split trains 8 of 11 (no hamstrings, calves or side delts) and would read 8/11 = 73% and not fire, on exactly the program the note exists for.
2. **Three variants, one chosen by engine state, in this order:** a muscle's one session is over the per-session cap (`frequencyDistribution.perSessionSetCap`, 10) names the muscle and its sets; else the engine's whole-program total is under `programStructure.minWeeklySets` ("for volume specifically, add a second training day rather than cram more sets into your current days"); else informational ("one session is not disqualifying if the per-session volume is right"). The first sentence says "Every muscle" only when all muscles are once a week, "Every muscle you train" when every trained one is, and "Most muscles" otherwise.
3. **Unscored.** Not a finding, strength, attention area, biggest opportunity or input to the Fit band. No weight, band, severity, leverage, threshold or evidence claim moved; engine stays 0.6.1; calibration "no change"; coach parity 21/24 with the same pass set. It is computed in `check.analyze` (`observation`) and rendered by `AssessmentDisplay` only when given, between the Overall block and Strengths. Only `/check` passes one.
4. **The copy never calls once a week a defect** (tested: no "only", "too few", "bad", "wrong"), and all variants pass `FORBIDDEN_COPY_PATTERNS`.

**Reconciled with the Actions (same session).** Variant B says that, for volume specifically, the fix is to add a training day "rather than cram more sets into your current days", and the whole-program volume action used to name sets on the listed days. When the observation fires, `check.analyze` now passes `addDay` to `nameActions`, and that action proposes a training day instead: the lowest muscles (up to 4), 3 sets each, run through `ADD_WORKOUT_DAY` and the engine, so the after-numbers and the week's total are the engine's ("Add a training day: 3 sets of Lateral Raise, 3 of Dumbbell Row ... → side delts 3, ... sets/week. The week then has 27 hard sets; the general floor is 40."). For a 2-3 day program the rotation stays first ("Run your 3 days twice a week → 48 hard sets a week. Or add a training day: ..."), since a second exposure per muscle is what the note recommends. The day is stated honestly: it may still leave the week under the floor, and the sentence says so. Default off, so the Builder, Review and every other `nameActions` caller are unchanged. Where no day can be built the action falls back to the older additions.
**Scoped, not contradictory (founder, 2026-11-11).** Variant B now reads "...for volume specifically, the fix is to add a second training day rather than cram more sets into your current days", so the advice is about the total. A pattern-gap action such as "Add 3 sets of Romanian Deadlift on Legs" answers a different problem (a missing hinge) and reads as complementary.

Rejected: a scored FREQUENCY finding (contradicts the profile's Tier 1-2 claim; changes calibration and parity); firing only when volume is also short (misses the bro split at 7 chest sets).
