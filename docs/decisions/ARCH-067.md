### ARCH-067 — /check Session F: days a week, sign-up copy, an example, an honest About
Date: 2026-11-08 | Status: Accepted (founder direction, 2026-11-08) | Reversible: Yes. Display, copy, one optional query field and one optional options argument; nothing is stored and `AssessmentSnapshot` is untouched.
Addendum to ARCH-065. **Supersedes** its "days-per-week question on `/check` itself" deferral, its "the About claim about the coaches is unchanged" line, and D2's "no pricing claim". `routineParser.ts` cites an "ARCH-066" (real pastes) that has no decision file; this record does not stand in for it.

Context: the independent audit of 2026-11-06 scored the assessment 5/10 and sign-up 3/10 and answered No. Its open items were that the checker never asks how many days a week the lifter trains, that the sign-up page does not say what the account is, that `/check` shows nothing of what a result looks like, that the About page reads against the banner, and (found afterwards) that spreadsheet-style `1a.` list labels turn every line into a question.

**No engine change.** No `HYPERTROPHY_CONFIG` value, band, severity, weight or leverage moved (engine stays 0.6.1); no schema, no `packages/ai`. `calibration.test.ts` and coach parity (21/24, same pass set) are unchanged because every new argument is absent on those paths.

Decision:
1. **Days a week is an input (Fix 1).** `/check` asks "How many days a week do you train?" (1-7; default is the days parsed from the routine, so the default never changes a result). It travels as `/check/<hash>?d=N`, only when it differs from the days listed, so the hash (the program) stays byte-identical, old links still work, and the pending-program save is untouched. `check.analyze` takes `{hash, daysPerWeek?}` and, when it differs from the listed training days, passes `AnalysisContext.daysPerWeek` (read since Session C1 by VOLUME, FREQUENCY and the PROGRAM_STRUCTURE attendance rule) to `computeAnalysis` and `nameActions`. Two additive, default-off arguments keep every number in the same week: `simulate(..., {context})` and `nameActions(..., {context})`, so an action's "→ N sets/week" is read the way its finding is, and "Run your 3 days twice a week" is withheld once the lifter has said how many days they train. Omitted, output is identical. The split tab's selector defaults to "As the split lists it", because a split is one click and its day count is not known before the click. **The pre-paid-tier requirement (founder D5, 2026-11-06) is met.**
   - *Known limitation:* the cross-muscle ratios still read the listed days (`analysis/runsPerWeek.ts` header). A 3-day program run on 6 days gets doubled volume and frequency but its ratio lines (press 12 / pull 10) are per listed week.
   - The result says "3 days listed, assessed as 6 days a week" so a shared link states its assumption.
2. **The sign-up page says what the account is (Fix 3).** Heading "Save your N-exercise program." from `/check` with a count (`&n=`, validated 1-105, or derived from a decoding `h`), "Save your program." from `/check` without one, "Create your account." with no program to save. A lead ("Track your training in one place... Free to use, no credit card, export anytime."), three bullets (a target for every lift each session, progress with charts, personal records and first-vs-now, adjusting the program without starting over; each exists in the app), and "Already have an account? Sign in →". The result's sign-up button carries "Free — save your program and check it again after edits." **This replaces D2's "no pricing, trial or limit claim" with a present-tense one: free to use, no credit card.** If a paid tier ships, a separate notice on this page at that time still applies. Sign-up inputs are 44px on phones, scoped to the form.
3. **An example assessment on `/check` (Fix 2).** A static, labelled "Example" block above the paste box with three cards in the product's own wording. `copy.test.ts` holds the example to the profile (14 hard sets is in the Adequate band; 6 of 6 patterns reads Balanced) and to the real renderer (the balance line equals `ratioLines` for 12 and 10). "Faded" is a muted background and dashed border, not CSS `opacity`, which would dim the text and fail the contrast check. All three cards are favourable; the label says it is an example, and a real result often leads with a gap.
4. **About says what was tested and no more (Fix 4).** "Tested against 24 sample programs. 21 match our prediction of what a strength coach would say; 3 are documented exceptions awaiting real coach input. Real coach review is ongoing. If you've seen a different analysis from a strength coach, we want to hear about it. The engine's thresholds are provisional — see evidence on any analysis." Why: the real five-coach review (2026-10-30) covered 6 of the 25 programs (B, and the five goal-dependent ones); every other expected headline in `expected.ts` is a prediction by the audit author, who is not a certified coach. So "reviewed by 5 coaches" and "match what a coach would say first" would have overstated it. The second "no expert has signed them off" sentence is gone from About (the banner on every assessment carries it, ARCH-046/065). **The invitation has no mailto**: the founder chose to publish no address until there is a feedback route, so a reader has no way to answer it yet. The exceptions bullets were dropped because the new text does not carry them.
5. **List labels are not exercise names (Fix 5).** `cleanLine` strips `1a.`, `1b.`, `23.`, `1)`, `a.`, `B.`, `(1)` and `(1a)`. A dot or bracket and a space are required, so `3x10`, `T-Bar Row` and `Day 1.` are untouched.

Rejected or deferred: a "days" field in the hash (changes every share link); scaling the cross-muscle ratios by `runsPerWeek` (an engine change); a feedback route (needs the founder's address and a place to read it); persisting days on a saved program (a schema change).

Consequence: all new static copy is in `CHECK_COPY` and under `FORBIDDEN_COPY_PATTERNS` via `allCheckCopy()`. Tests whose premise changed carry no loosened assertion: `check.spec.ts` (the sign-up URL now carries `&n=`; the About assertions), and the `rotationHint` sentence ("paste it as 6 days") is replaced because the selector now answers it.
