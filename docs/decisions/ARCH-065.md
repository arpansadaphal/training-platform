### ARCH-065 — /check audit response: display-layer and wording fixes, no engine change
Date: 2026-11-06 | Status: Accepted (founder decisions D1-D5, 2026-11-06) | Reversible: Yes. Every change is a display rule, a copy string or a post-step beside the Assessment; nothing is stored and `AssessmentSnapshot` is untouched.
Addendum to ARCH-063 (engine output) and ARCH-064 (public checker). Amends the copy of ARCH-046/054 (the provisional banner) and the Session E claim that `/check` renders `AssessmentDisplay` unchanged.

Context: a stranger audit of `/check` (`docs/handoffs/2026-11-06-check-stranger-test.md`) scored the assessment 5/10 and sign-up motivation 3/10. The engine's findings were right; what a lifter read was not: an action that cited a range and ended below it, strengths that were the floor of the range or praise for assumed effort, a headline that never said which muscles were behind "under-dosed", four questions about obvious lifts, and a banner and sign-up box that explained nothing.

**No engine change.** No `HYPERTROPHY_CONFIG` value, band, severity, weight, leverage or version moved (engine stays 0.6.1). `calibration.test.ts` reads "no change" on every program after every commit and coach parity stays 21/24 with the same pass and fail sets. No schema, no `packages/ai`.

Decision:
1. **Named actions reach the range they cite (Fix 1).** An addition is sized to reach the start of the Adequate band. Up to 4 sets go on one day; a muscle that needs 5-8 is split over the two best days ("3 sets of Cable Lateral Raise on Push and 2 on Pull → side delts 6.5 sets/week"), the after-number read off one engine run over both additions through the shared `applyMutation` (invariant 2). The day is the one that already trains the muscle (credited sets from the existing exercises), then the same body region, then the shortest session. A half-credit muscle says "Each set counts as half for upper back", and is **not named** when even its capped addition (6 sets) cannot reach the range: the line falls back to "Add weekly volume for X" instead of citing a number that ends under the line it quotes.
2. **Rotation (Fix 3b, founder D1).** The whole-program volume action may lead with "Run your 3 days twice a week → 48 hard sets a week." The 48 is the engine's: the program is analysed again with `AnalysisContext.daysPerWeek` doubled, the input Session C1 made VOLUME read. Offered only for 2-3 listed days, only when every muscle's FREQUENCY metric is at most 1 (a 3-day full-body program run twice is six full-body days, not a rotation), and only when the doubled week reaches the profile's whole-program floor (`programStructure.minWeeklySets`). Otherwise the additions stand alone. **Pre-paid-tier requirement (founder D5): a days-per-week input must ship before any charge.** Until then the paste view says "List every day you train. If you repeat a 3-day rotation twice a week, paste it as 6 days."
3. **Which muscles are behind "under-dosed" (Fix 3a).** `programVolumeDetail` (display copy) lists the Low muscles the whole-program finding absorbed, grouped by sets ("Quads, chest, upper back, hamstrings and glutes get 3 hard sets a week; side delts and rear delts 1.5; calves 0."). When every FREQUENCY metric is at most 1 it adds "No muscle gets 2 or more hard sets on more than one day." This is the engine's own FREQUENCY measure, said in words.
4. **Frequency is not made a finding. Considered and rejected.** The audit's "frequency is missing" would put a new finding on a once-a-week split. The profile cites evidence (Schoenfeld 2019; Pelland 2026) that frequency has no independent effect once weekly volume is equal, and a frequency finding would contradict it. The legitimate complaint (a coach names "once a week") is answered by stating the fact (3a) and by offering more sessions as the way to add volume (3b), not by a new axis or rollup.
5. **Each action says which finding it fixes (Fix 2).** The display prefixes an action with its finding's label ("Hinge · exercise selection: Add 3 sets of Romanian Deadlift ..."). The "Start with:" prefix is gone. Actions were already ordered headline-first; the audit's "the first action is not the biggest opportunity" was a reading of the prefix, not of the order.
6. **Obvious lifts are not questions, and the first guess is not a curl (Fix 5a).** A bare Bench, Squat, Row or Deadlift resolves to the standard barbell lift and is shown by its full name in the parsed list. Candidates are ranked with the usual lifts first, so "Incline" offers Incline Dumbbell Press before Incline Curl (the old pre-highlighted answer, a biceps exercise on a chest day, silently corrupted the assessment) and "Curl" offers Dumbbell Curl first. "Curl" is still asked.
7. **Strengths shown on `/check` are earned (Fix 4).** `displayStrengths` is display only and applied through a `checkMode` prop on `AssessmentDisplay`; every other surface shows the engine's strengths as before, and `Assessment.strengths`, the bands, severity, leverage and Fit are untouched (invariant 8). A volume strength needs the 10+ sets the profile already names as its target band; exercise selection counts only with all required patterns covered (5 of 6 is tolerated, not praised); effort is never credited as a strength because every pasted program is assumed RPE 8. The 10 is a Tier 3 presentation convention, not an engine threshold. **This reverses Session E's "the assessment is rendered unchanged" for `/check` only.**
8. **Words a lifter reads.**
   - Banner (founder D3): "Provisional: thresholds come from published research and coaching practice, graded by strength of evidence; no expert has signed them off yet. See evidence." Same fact as ARCH-046/054, still mounted only inside `AssessmentDisplay`, non-dismissable, never "validated" in the positive; the tier-by-tier detail is under "See evidence". Supersedes the ARCH-054 banner string.
   - Sign-up box (founder D2): says the account is free and lists three things it does, each checked against the app: the builder re-runs the check as you edit, a target for every lift each session from what you logged, and a journal, progress view and CSV export. No pricing, trial or limit claim. If a paid tier ships, that is a separate notice on the sign-up page at that time.
   - `/check` intro says what the visitor gets. The About page leads with what the checker does and no longer reads against the banner. **The About claim about the coaches ("tested against 24 test programs, reviewed by 5 coaches. 21 match the headline the coaches gave.") is unchanged and not strengthened; it is still the founder's to confirm (Session E handoff, manual action 4).**
   - On `/check` the engine's `Metric / Weight / Severity / Leverage` table is hidden (a `plain` prop on the cards); per-muscle sets stay under Details for grouped findings.

Rejected or deferred (and why): result-URL length (a shareable, stateless link is the design); the "Bro Split" title shown as "Could not parse" and Delete leaving the text in the box (Delete only dismisses the card; removing the text shifts the line numbers the answers are keyed by, so it needs a redesign, not a patch); the days-per-week question on `/check` itself (changes the share-link format; see item 2); engine-level strength rules (graded severity, E4); choosing the muscles the volume action names (Session D's two largest deficits stands).

Consequence: all new static copy is under `FORBIDDEN_COPY_PATTERNS` (`CHECK_COPY`, `PROVISIONAL_BANNER_*`, and the sentences from `namedActions`/`programVolumeDetail` are lint-tested). Tests whose premise changed carry an audit note and no assertion was loosened: `namedActions.test.ts` (upper back is no longer named when it cannot reach the range; "Start with:"), `routineParser.test.ts` (a bare "Bench" is no longer the ambiguous line), `check.spec.ts` (same, and the sign-up sentence), `regression.spec.ts` (banner wording).
