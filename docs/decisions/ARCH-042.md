### ARCH-042 — Grounding failures: bounded regeneration, then structured partial

Date: 2026-09-27 | Status: FROZEN | Reversible: Yes, contained to `packages/ai/src/orchestrator.ts`, `packages/ai/src/grounding.ts`, and the `grounding_warning` variant of `AIMessageSegment`

Decision: On a grounding failure, the orchestrator re-prompts the provider once with the specific offending tokens named and asks for a corrected response. If the retry also fails, it drops the offending segments, keeps the grounded ones, and appends a `grounding_warning` segment. The whole turn does not fail; the user sees the parts of the response that could be trusted plus a visible statement that content was withheld. A number is never silently dropped — a claim is either present and grounded, or withheld with a warning. The failure is logged to Sentry with the offending claim text and the model's original segment for debugging. The retry budget is shared across both validation axes: `MAX_ATTEMPTS = 2` in the orchestrator, one retry per turn total, not one per axis.

Rationale: Two source documents disagreed. `10-ai-coach-architecture.md` described the grounding check as "recommended hardening, not MVP-blocking," implying a log-only treatment. The Phase 8 briefing required that a grounding failure "return a structured error or regenerate," implying a gate. Neither reading is compatible with the other, and leaving the conflict open would let whichever implementation landed first quietly become policy. A hard gate (fail the whole turn) would fail entire conversational turns on false-positive grounding checks — a wrong outcome for a surface where most turns are prose with one or two numbers. A log-only treatment would leave an ungrounded number visible to the user, which is what Final Freeze invariant 1 ("the deterministic engine is the sole source of quantitative truth") exists to prevent. The bounded-retry-plus-partial shape lets the model self-correct once, then degrades visibly without taking the whole turn down — stricter than log-only, more practical than whole-turn failure.

Alternatives considered: (a) Hard gate — regenerate once, then fail the whole turn with a structured error. Rejected: false-positive grounding checks would kill otherwise-good turns. (b) Log-only — record the failure and return the response unmodified. Rejected: violates invariant 1 by letting an ungrounded number reach the user. (c) Silent drop — remove the offending segments without a visible marker. Rejected explicitly by guidance F ("must not silently drop the number").

Consequence: `grounding_warning` is part of the persisted `AIMessageSegment` union and the client renderer. A future change that increases the retry budget, or that changes partial-failure handling (e.g. to fail the whole turn on retry exhaustion), requires a new entry superseding this one. The shared-retry-budget decision means a turn that fails both validation and grounding gets exactly one corrective attempt total, not one per axis.

Source: `10-ai-coach-architecture.md` ("Grounding and hallucination prevention"); `phases/phase-08-ai-coach.md`; Phase 8 kickoff exchange Q7 (revised).
