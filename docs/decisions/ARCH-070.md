### ARCH-070 — /check: "Weekly sets per muscle"
Date: 2026-11-11 | Status: Accepted (founder direction, 2026-11-11) | Reversible: Yes. Display only; reads the VOLUME axes the assessment already holds; nothing stored; `AssessmentSnapshot` untouched. No engine, config, schema or `packages/ai` change; calibration and coach parity (21/24) unchanged.
Addendum to ARCH-065 (earned strengths) and ARCH-069.

Context: all four independent reports of Sessions F and G asked for a per-muscle view, and two reported that chest, shoulders, calves or arms never appear ("is it fine, or not checked?"). **Cause (verified):** a result mentioned a muscle only in a finding (Low muscles) or as an earned strength (`displayStrengths`: 10+ sets). A muscle that was Adequate at 6-9 sets was in neither, so it appeared nowhere. The same reports flagged numbers that disagreed on one screen.

Decision:
1. **A table of every muscle the engine counted, public checker only.** One row per VOLUME axis: muscle, weekly hard sets, the profile's band name (Low, Adequate, High, Excessive) and a bar over a 0-30 track with the Adequate range (6-20, read from the profile's bands) shaded. Order is chest to calves (`MUSCLE_DISPLAY_ORDER`). The bar is decoration; the number and band name carry the meaning (a real `<table>`, axe clean). Heading line: "N of M muscles are in the 6-20 range."
2. **One number per muscle.** The figures are the engine's VOLUME metric (a set also counts toward the muscles it helps), the same ones the finding cards and Fit band use, so triceps reads 3.5 in the whole-program card and in the table. Nothing is recounted or regraded (`muscleVolumeView` is a read).
3. **It replaces the per-muscle volume strength cards on `/check`.** Six green cards that said the same thing, minus the muscles fine at 6-9 sets, are gone; what remains under Strengths (the movement-pattern card) and the "N strengths" count are what is shown. The "earned" 10-set rule (ARCH-065 Fix 4) still decides which strengths are cards; it just no longer hides muscles. Builder and Review are unchanged (gated on `checkMode`).
4. **Placed after Actions** (before the balance lines), so the verdict and the fix come first and the table is the evidence.
5. **The balance lines still count differently, and now say so.** They count only sets that train a muscle directly (arms 12 vs biceps 13 and triceps 13; hamstrings 9 vs 12). Where the balance section is on the page (nothing flagged) the footnote says its numbers can be lower; where it is not (findings exist) the footnote does not mention it. **Not resolved:** the two methods still disagree on that page, explained rather than removed. Dropping the balance lines from `/check`, or showing the direct count in the table too, would remove it; neither was asked for.

Rejected: a "per-muscle" sort by deficit (a lifter looks up a muscle by where it is); showing only muscles under the range (the point is that the fine ones are listed); recomputing direct sets in the table (a second counting method on one screen).
