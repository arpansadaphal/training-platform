TOP 30 CUES — FOR FOUNDER REVIEW (not in the app, not committed)
Picked by how many of the six /check presets use the exercise (ranks 1-25 are in 2+ presets). Ranks 26-30 are single-preset picks (Dumbbell Row, Dumbbell Curl, Push-Up, Plank, Cable Chest Fly); swap any.
Lint: 0 violations on the shared forbidden-copy list + injur/prevent/safe/protect/pain/hurt/spine + build/maxim/target/grow/gain.
Word budgets: Setup <=20, Action <=20, Feel <=12. Exceptions are the two founder-written Feel lines marked below.

1. Leg Press
   Setup:  Feet shoulder-width on the middle of the platform, hips flat on the seat, lower back against the pad.
   Action: Lower until your knees travel toward your chest, then drive the platform away through your whole foot.
   Feel:   Feel your quads and glutes pushing, not your toes.

2. Incline Dumbbell Press
   Setup:  Bench at 30°, dumbbells at your shoulders, shoulder blades pinned back into the pad.
   Action: Press the dumbbells up and slightly together over your upper chest, then lower under control.
   Feel:   Feel your upper chest stretch at the bottom, squeeze at the top.

3. Lateral Raise   [founder text, verbatim (Feel is 14 words, budget 12)]
   Setup:  Stand tall, slight forward lean, arms angled 30° in front of your body.
   Action: Lead with your elbows, push the dumbbells out toward the walls.
   Feel:   Tension strictly on the side of your shoulders. Traps stay relaxed — no shrug.

4. Back Squat
   Setup:  Bar across your upper back, feet shoulder-width, toes turned slightly out, chest up.
   Action: Sit straight down between your hips, then push the floor away and stand up through your mid-foot.
   Feel:   Feel your quads and glutes drive you up out of the bottom.

5. Barbell Bench Press
   Setup:  Eyes under the bar, shoulder blades pinned back into the bench, feet planted flat on the floor.
   Action: Lower the bar to the same spot on your chest, then press it up and slightly back.
   Feel:   Feel your chest and triceps pushing the bar away from you.

6. Barbell Curl
   Setup:  Stand tall, bar at your thighs, palms forward, elbows tucked against your sides.
   Action: Curl the bar toward your shoulders with your elbows pinned in place, then lower slowly.
   Feel:   Feel your biceps squeeze at the top and stretch at the bottom.

7. Hammer Curl
   Setup:  Stand tall with the dumbbells at your sides, palms facing each other, elbows tucked in.
   Action: Curl the dumbbells up like you're lifting hammers, wrists still, then lower slowly.
   Feel:   Feel the outside of your upper arm and your forearms working.

8. Leg Curl
   Setup:  Lie face down, pad just above your heels, hips pressed flat into the bench.
   Action: Curl your heels toward your glutes, then lower slowly until your legs are nearly straight.
   Feel:   Feel your hamstrings squeeze at the top, stretch on the way down.

9. Overhead Press
   Setup:  Bar at your collarbone, hands just outside your shoulders, glutes squeezed, ribs down.
   Action: Press the bar straight up and push your head through once it clears your face.
   Feel:   Feel your shoulders and triceps drive the bar to lockout.

10. Pull-Up
   Setup:  Hang from the bar, hands just outside your shoulders, shoulder blades pulled down away from your ears.
   Action: Drive your elbows down toward your hips and pull your chest up to meet the bar.
   Feel:   Feel your lats squeeze under your armpits at the top.

11. Romanian Deadlift   [founder text, verbatim (Feel is 22 words, budget 12). Action says the bar "drags up" your legs while the hips go back; on the way down it slides DOWN. Suggest "slides down".]
   Setup:  Feet hip-width, knees soft, bar against your shins.
   Action: Push your hips back like you're closing a car door with your butt, bar drags up your legs.
   Feel:   Feel the stretch along the back of your thighs. Stop when the hamstrings are taut, not when the bar hits the floor.

12. Tricep Pushdown
   Setup:  Stand close to the cable, elbows pinned at your sides, hands on the handle.
   Action: Push the handle down until your arms are straight, elbows staying put, then let it rise slowly.
   Feel:   Feel your triceps squeeze at the bottom.

13. Seated Cable Row
   Setup:  Feet on the platform, knees slightly bent, chest tall, handle at arm's length.
   Action: Pull the handle to your stomach, leading with your elbows, then reach forward slowly.
   Feel:   Feel your shoulder blades squeeze together and your mid-back working.

14. Barbell Row
   Setup:  Hinge forward from your hips, bar hanging below your chest, hands just outside your knees.
   Action: Pull the bar toward your lower ribs, leading with your elbows, then lower it under control.
   Feel:   Feel your lats and upper back squeeze as the bar reaches you.

15. Standing Calf Raise
   Setup:  Balls of your feet on the edge, shoulders under the pads, knees soft.
   Action: Rise onto your toes as high as you can, pause, then lower your heels below the edge.
   Feel:   Feel a deep stretch in your calves at the bottom.

16. Dumbbell Shoulder Press
   Setup:  Seat upright, dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward, ribs down.
   Action: Press the dumbbells straight up until your arms are extended, then lower to shoulder height.
   Feel:   Feel the front and sides of your shoulders pushing.

17. Lat Pulldown   [founder text, verbatim]
   Setup:  Snug the thigh pad. Grip just outside shoulders, shoulder blades pulled down away from your ears.
   Action: Drive your elbows down toward your hips, chest lifted to meet the bar.
   Feel:   Squeeze your armpits tight at the bottom — no shoulder roll.

18. Face Pull
   Setup:  Cable at face height, rope in both hands, step back until the weight hangs off the stack.
   Action: Pull the rope toward your face and pull the ends apart, elbows high.
   Feel:   Feel the backs of your shoulders squeeze, with your traps relaxed.

19. Bulgarian Split Squat
   Setup:  Back foot on a bench behind you, front foot a long step forward, torso tall.
   Action: Lower straight down until your front thigh is about parallel, then push through your front foot.
   Feel:   Feel your front quad and glute working, not your back foot.

20. Conventional Deadlift
   Setup:  Bar over your mid-foot, hands just outside your legs, shoulders slightly in front of the bar.
   Action: Push the floor away and keep the bar dragging up your legs until you stand tall.
   Feel:   Feel your glutes and hamstrings drive your hips forward at the top.

21. Dumbbell Bench Press
   Setup:  Lie back with the dumbbells at your chest, feet planted, shoulder blades pinned into the bench.
   Action: Press the dumbbells up over your shoulders, then lower until you feel your chest stretch.
   Feel:   Feel your chest squeeze as the dumbbells come together over you.

22. Glute Bridge
   Setup:  Lie on your back, feet flat and hip-width, knees bent, arms by your sides.
   Action: Push through your heels and drive your hips up until your shoulders, hips and knees line up.
   Feel:   Squeeze your glutes hard at the top.

23. Goblet Squat
   Setup:  Hold one dumbbell against your chest with both hands, elbows pointing down, feet just wider than hips.
   Action: Sit down between your knees with your elbows inside them, then stand up through your mid-foot.
   Feel:   Feel your quads and glutes working as you stand.

24. Leg Extension
   Setup:  Seat set so your knees line up with the machine's pivot, pad just above your ankles.
   Action: Press your legs out until your knees are straight, then lower slowly.
   Feel:   Feel your quads squeeze at the top.

25. Skull Crusher
   Setup:  Lie back, bar over your chest on straight arms, upper arms vertical.
   Action: Bend only your elbows to lower the bar toward your forehead, then press it back up.
   Feel:   Feel your triceps stretch at the bottom and squeeze as you press.

26. Dumbbell Row
   Setup:  One hand and knee on a bench, dumbbell hanging under your shoulder, back parallel to the floor.
   Action: Pull your elbow back toward your hip, then lower until your arm is straight.
   Feel:   Feel your lats squeeze at the top and stretch at the bottom.

27. Dumbbell Curl
   Setup:  Stand tall, dumbbells at your sides, palms forward, elbows tucked against your ribs.
   Action: Curl the dumbbells up to your shoulders while your elbows stay in place.
   Feel:   Feel your biceps squeeze at the top.

28. Push-Up
   Setup:  Hands just outside your shoulders, feet together, body in one line from head to heels.
   Action: Lower your chest to a fist from the floor, then push the floor away.
   Feel:   Feel your chest and triceps pushing you up.

29. Plank
   Setup:  Forearms on the floor, elbows under your shoulders, toes down, body in one line.
   Action: Squeeze your glutes and brace your abs as if a light punch is coming, then hold.
   Feel:   Feel your abs tight and your hips level.

30. Cable Chest Fly
   Setup:  Stand between the cables, one foot forward, soft bend in your elbows, handles at chest height.
   Action: Sweep your hands together in a wide arc in front of your chest, then open slowly.
   Feel:   Feel your chest stretch as you open, squeeze as you meet.
