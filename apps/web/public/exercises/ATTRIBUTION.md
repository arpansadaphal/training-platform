# Exercise drawings

These WebP files are converted copies of drawings from Everkinetic
(https://github.com/everkinetic/data), created by Greg Priday and contributors,
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes: vector drawings were rasterised; every drawing was scaled to fit a
480-pixel square on a white background and saved as WebP. Nothing was redrawn.

The converted files are shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0. The source
commit, the exercise-to-drawing mapping and the conversion script are in
`apps/web/scripts/illustrations/` (the commit is pinned in `mapping.ts`).
`<slug>-1.webp` and `<slug>-2.webp` are the two frames of one drawing.
